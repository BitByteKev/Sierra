The hedge separated the property from a busy cross-street and had gone hollow on the inside from years of repeated buzz-trimming. We took it back in three stages over the course of a year — first reducing height, then thinning the interior to let light reach inner branches, finally re-shaping. By month twelve it was dense floor-to-top again.

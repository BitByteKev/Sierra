A coastal La Jolla home that wanted curb appeal without the water bill. We removed declining fescue, regraded for drainage, laid weed barrier and decomposed granite paths, and planted a layered palette of agave, salvia, and dwarf olive. All four front irrigation zones were converted from spray to drip.

The lawn had been on the same mid-day schedule for years and most of it was bare or compacted. We aerated, top-dressed with compost, overseeded with a shade-tolerant Tall Fescue blend, and installed a Rachio 3 controller. By the eight-week mark, the lawn was full coverage and the irrigation runtime had been cut by a third.

A water-conscious homeowner whose system was wasting most of its output on hardscape overspray. We replaced spray heads with point-source drip emitters, installed a Hunter Hydrawise controller, and submitted the SoCal Water$mart rebate paperwork on their behalf. The first post-conversion water bill was 38% lower than the prior year for the same period.

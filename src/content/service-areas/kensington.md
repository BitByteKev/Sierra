Kensington is one of San Diego's most architecturally consistent neighborhoods, and that translates to landscaping: there's a strong unspoken expectation that the front yard fits the era of the house. We work with that. Spanish revival lines pair beautifully with olive, lavender, and salvia. Tudor-style homes look right with formal boxwood and yew hedges. We rarely recommend tearing out mature plantings here — most of what looks tired can be brought back with two or three seasons of intentional pruning and feeding.

La Jolla is one of San Diego's most demanding microclimates for landscaping. The marine layer keeps mornings cool and damp, then the afternoon sun hits hard — which means plant choices that work in inland neighborhoods like North Park can struggle here. We default to a salt-tolerant, sun-resilient palette for west-facing lots: agave, kangaroo paw, lavender, manzanita, and dwarf olive.

We've worked extensively in Bird Rock, La Jolla Shores, La Jolla Village, and the Muirlands. Hillside drainage is the issue that comes up most often — water that's only a nuisance on a flat lot becomes a serious erosion problem on a La Jolla slope, and most of our installs here include a regrading conversation.

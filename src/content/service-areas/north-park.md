The classic North Park bungalow lot is narrow, deeper than it is wide, with one or two mature trees that dominate the yard. The shade those trees throw is a gift and a constraint — most cool-season turf does fine, but warm-season Bermuda or Kikuyu will struggle. We default to a shade-tolerant Tall Fescue blend for lawns here and lean into shade-loving understory plantings like Mexican sage, hellebore, and fern for beds.

Walkability matters in North Park too: front yards are seen up close, every day, by everyone in the neighborhood. We design for that — clean edges, intentional plantings, real curb appeal — not the "pretty from the curb at 35 mph" approach that works in larger-lot neighborhoods.

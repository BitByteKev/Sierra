faqs:
  - q: When is the best time to trim hedges in San Diego?
    a: It varies. Most evergreen hedges respond best to a hard cut in early spring and a touch-up in mid-summer. Flowering shrubs get cut right after they bloom. We schedule each plant on its own clock, not all at once.
  - q: Can you fix a hedge that's gone too tall or hollow inside?
    a: Often, yes — but it usually takes two or three sessions across a year, not one heavy cut. We'll walk you through the plan.

A bad trim is obvious for months. A good one disappears. We shape hedges and shrubs to look intentional from the moment we leave — never the "buzz-cut" look — and we time cuts so the plant heals into a fuller form, not a thinner one.

For larger trees or anything requiring climbing, we partner with a licensed arborist and coordinate the scheduling for you.

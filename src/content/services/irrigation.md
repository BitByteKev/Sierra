Half the irrigation work in San Diego is undoing what someone else installed in a hurry. We don't do that. Every install starts with a coverage map, a pressure test, and a conversation about how your property gets sun across the day. The result is a system that uses less water and keeps plants healthier — not just one that turns on when scheduled.

For repairs, we carry common Hunter, Rain Bird, and Toro parts on the truck so most issues get fixed on the first visit.

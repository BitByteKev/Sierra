Sierra Verde Landscaping treats lawn care as the foundation of a healthy property — not a chore to rush through. Our maintenance routes are scheduled tightly so the same crew sees your yard every visit and learns what works for your specific turf and soil.

We start every new client relationship with a walk-through: we ID the grass type (most San Diego lawns are a mix of Tall Fescue, Bermuda, or Kikuyu), check your irrigation coverage, and set a mowing height that protects the crown of the plant from summer heat stress. From there it's reliable, on-schedule service with no shortcuts.

faqs:
  - q: When should I prune my citrus trees in San Diego?
    a: Light shaping cuts after the spring flush. Avoid heavy pruning during summer — citrus bark sunburns easily on exposed limbs in San Diego's afternoon sun.
  - q: My rose bushes look exhausted. Can they be saved?
    a: Almost always. A proper dormant-season hard prune plus a feeding and mulch refresh in early spring brings back most roses within a single growing cycle.

Pruning is the part of landscaping where small mistakes show up months later. We prune one plant at a time, by hand, using sharp clean tools — and we know which species respond to which cuts at which time of year. It's slower than buzzing through with hedge trimmers, and the difference is visible by the next growing season.

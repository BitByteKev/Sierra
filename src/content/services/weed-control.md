Weed control isn't a single product. It's a maintenance rhythm. We combine mechanical removal, well-timed pre-emergents, and mulch depth to keep your beds clean without the constant spray-cycle you see from larger crews. If you want a fully organic program, just ask — we have an organic-only option that works in San Diego's climate.
